# Client brief (as received)

The ministry's own brief for the RCN North America work, pasted verbatim. The PRD (`02-PRD.md`)
expands on it; where the two differ, the notes in `04-open-items.md` say which one the build follows.

## 1. RCN North America (rcnnorthamerica.com)
The network homepage for every branch in the US and Canada:

- Hero: who RCN North America is and the mandate
- Find a branch: a map or list of all locations
- Upcoming national conferences, e.g. Marching Orders, with registration
- Sermon library with filters by branch, speaker and series
- Prayer request form and plan-a-visit form, routed to the right branch
- A Give section where each branch has its own giving link
- A tech & systems team interest form
- Links to the AAO site, RCN Global, YouTube and Telegram
- Inspiration: vineyardusa.org, life.church, hillsong.com

## 2. RCN Boston (/boston)
A branch page built from the template:

- Pointman: Minister Peter Mitch
- Service times, address and map, parking and transit
- A plan-a-visit form
- Local events and the weekly schedule
- Boston sermons pulled from the main library
- Prayer/contact form, Give, and the WhatsApp/Telegram group link
- Reference: parkstreet.org

## 3. RCN Florida / Orlando (/florida)
The same template, extended:

- Pointman: Minister Mica-El Mumba (this is his own branch, so it'll get the closest look)
- Orlando, Saturday 9 AM to 4 PM
- Weekly classes with join links: Monday Discipleship, Wednesday Foundations, Friday Prayer
- 7 Hours Apostolic Gathering: the next date, what to expect, RSVP via ChMeetings
- Kids (newborn to 15), serve departments, events
- Prayer/contact form, Give
- Its own RCN Florida logo
